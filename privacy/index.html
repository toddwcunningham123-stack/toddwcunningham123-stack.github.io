Privacy

Last updated August 26, 2026

This covers the iOS apps Kelp, DUET, and Marble Music by Todd Cunningham II. This is not the Swedish company Aqua Mobilis AB.

What we collect

The apps do not create accounts. They do not have their own servers. Audio is processed on your device. Projects and sessions stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

Kelp

Kelp has no ads and no in-app purchases. It does not use advertising identifiers.

DUET and Marble Music

These apps are free and show a small banner ad from Google AdMob. You can pay once, through Apple, to remove ads forever. Apple processes that purchase. We never see your payment details.

Google may collect device information, IP address, and advertising identifiers to show ads. We ask Google for non-personalized ads. Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The apps are rated 4+. Kelp does not show ads. DUET and Marble Music show a banner from Google. If you do not want ads on a child’s device, use Remove Ads or do not install those two.
