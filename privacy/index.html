Privacy

Last updated August 27, 2026

This covers the iOS apps Kelp (including Live mode), DUET, Marble Music, Anemone, Tape Eater, Bloom, and Ghost98 by Todd Cunningham II. This is not the Swedish company Aqua Mobilis AB.

What we collect

The apps do not create accounts. They do not have their own servers. Audio and camera frames are processed on your device. Projects, sessions, and recordings stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

Kelp (including Live mode), Anemone, Tape Eater, Bloom, and Ghost98

These apps have no ads and no in-app purchases. They do not use advertising identifiers.

DUET and Marble Music

These apps are free and show a small banner ad from Google AdMob. You can pay once, through Apple, to remove ads forever. Apple processes that purchase. We never see your payment details.

Google may collect device information, IP address, and advertising identifiers to show ads. We ask Google for non-personalized ads. Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Camera and photos

Anemone uses the camera for on-device hand pose. Tape Eater uses the camera and can load and save video through Photos. Camera frames and video are processed on your device. They are not uploaded. Tape Eater does not use the microphone. Neither app has an account or its own servers.

Children

The apps are rated 4+. Ads appear only in DUET and Marble Music. Anemone and Tape Eater use the camera on your device. If you do not want ads on a child’s device, use Remove Ads or do not install those two.
