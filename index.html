Aqua Mobilis

Small music tools.

Kelp is a free loop studio. Kelp Live is a performance instrument. Duet is a two-player tap instrument. Marble Music is marbles, gravity, and notes. Anemone is a camera touch synth. Tape Eater is an analog video mangler.
